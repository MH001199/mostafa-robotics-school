# Teaching References

These resources informed the school’s structure and vocabulary. The lessons and exercises are written for this curriculum.

- [Adafruit Learning System](https://learn.adafruit.com/breadboards-for-beginners) shows how component photos, pin explanations, and practical wiring support each other.
- [SparkFun Inventor’s Kit](https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/sparkfun-inventors-kit-experiment-guide---v41/all) demonstrates progressive experiments with parts, wiring, and observable results.
- [The Odin Project Foundations](https://www.theodinproject.com/paths/foundations/courses/foundations) informed the progression from prerequisites to independent work.
- [GitHub Skills](https://skills.github.com/) gives students a place to practice contribution workflows.
- [Raspberry Pi Foundation PRIMM teaching guide](https://www.raspberrypi.org/teach/pedagogy) informed Predict, Run, Investigate, Modify, and Make.
